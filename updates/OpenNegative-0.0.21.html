Nothing in this version changes the rendering of a photo you have already adjusted. One fix, on the thumbnail cache alone. Your settings, your rolls and your presets are untouched.

Thumbnails survive an unplugged drive

  • Launching with the originals' drive disconnected no longer empties the thumbnail cache. The tidying pass built its list of claimed files by reading each original, and silently dropped every one it could not reach — so a whole library on an external drive was read as files nobody wanted, and deleted. Measured on a 632-frame library: the 592 frames on the external drive lost their thumbnails, the 40 on the internal drive kept theirs.
  • An unreachable original now keeps its files, claimed through the fingerprint the index holds, which survives the drive going away. And an index that does not yet account for every member of the library deletes nothing at all: without knowing who is a member, no file can be called an orphan.
  • The tidying itself still works. A superseded thumbnail of an original that really was read is still removed, and so are the files of a photo taken out of the library. The fix is a rule about proof, not the housekeeping switched off.

Version 0.0.20 announced that thumbnails had stopped being thrown away. That was half true: the rebuild stopped inventing fingerprints, but the pass that sweeps unclaimed cache files kept reading an unreachable original as an unwanted one. This is the other half.
